1 · Compare everyoneEvery pair of students gets a similarity score: how many questions they answered the same way.
2 · Try thousands of splitsThe algorithm shuffles people between teams (always 2–4 people), keeps changes that make teammates more alike, and tries every possible number of teams.
3 · Measure the adjustmentFor each student: who was their best possible match, and how much less alike is the team they got? Being split from every best match is a forced move.
